🌎 Transforming the Future: MIRA & Bogoboom
Innovative solutions created in Brazil 🇧🇷, built to change the world 🌍

At Tecnologia Santos, we don’t just create software — we create impact. We believe technology has the power to transform lives, bridge gaps, and solve deep-rooted global challenges. Two of our flagship projects — MIRA and Bogoboom — are a testimony to that belief.

MIRA is not just a job portal — it's a movement. Born in São Paulo and inspired by the desire to make the global tech industry more inclusive and accessible, MIRA connects highly skilled IT professionals from around the world with life-changing opportunities. It doesn’t matter if you're a seasoned engineer or a passionate newcomer — MIRA gives you the tools, visibility, and support to make your mark.

MIRA is free. Forever. Because we believe opportunity should never come with a price tag. Through a beautiful and intuitive platform, job seekers gain global visibility, and companies gain access to a pre-qualified, diverse, and eager talent pool. It’s more than recruiting — it’s community building. It’s empowerment. It’s the future of work, reimagined.

Now, enter Bogoboom — our smart relocation and housing platform created with love in Brazil and launched in Bogotá. For years, companies around the world have struggled with the hidden costs of relocating employees — from expensive hotel stays to overwhelmed HR teams. Bogoboom changes all that.

With Bogoboom, companies and individuals relocating to Bogotá get access to a curated network of verified real estate agencies offering affordable, long-term housing solutions. It saves money, eases stress, and accelerates onboarding — all while delivering a human-first experience.

But Bogoboom is more than convenience — it’s about dignity. It’s about giving someone moving to a new city — often a new country — a warm welcome and a solid foundation. It’s about helping companies support their people, and helping newcomers feel at home from day one. This platform was created because we care. Because we’ve been there. Because we know what it’s like to search for belonging in a new place.

Tecnologia Santos was founded on a dream — a Brazilian dream — to harness technology for good. Every line of code we write, every feature we launch, is infused with passion and purpose. We don’t just serve clients. We serve lives. We change stories. We open doors.

MIRA and Bogoboom are just the beginning. They are the seeds of a global transformation. One focused on opportunity, inclusion, connection, and trust. These are not just tools. They are movements. They are answers to real problems faced by real people. They are hope, made digital.
