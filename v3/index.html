Hotels are the expensive way to start.

Companies that hire from other cities often put new hires in hotels. It inflates relocation budgets, delays integration and stresses people who are still learning a new city.

With a hotel stay

  • You pay per night while the housing search drags on
  • New hires feel like visitors, not residents
  • HR coordinates by email and spreadsheet

With Bogoboom

  • A real home is lined up before arrival
  • New hires start their job already settled
  • HR follows progress in one place
