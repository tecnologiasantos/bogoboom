Bogoboom is your bridge to a smooth landing in any place in the world. Whether you're a newcomer, an HR department or a real estate agency, the platform will help you connect and thrive.

Hotels are the expensive way to start

Companies that hire from other cities often put new hires in hotels. That inflates relocation budgets, delays integration and stresses people who are still learning a new city. Bogoboom's idea is simple: a real, long-term home lined up before arrival.

With Bogoboom, your company's HR team will be able to:

  • ✅ Save money on hotel stays and short-term housing
  • ✅ Simplify employee relocation with real housing options
  • ✅ Reduce internal workload and stress
  • ✅ Offer a smoother, more professional onboarding
  • ✅ Strengthen the company's reputation as relocation-friendly
  • ✅ Tap into a curated network of vetted real estate agencies

Newcomers, this is how Bogoboom will make your move easier:

  • ✅ Explore verified listings from real estate professionals
  • ✅ Secure long-term housing before or when you arrive
  • ✅ Save time and avoid scams
  • ✅ Filter by your needs, lifestyle and budget
  • ✅ Register for free

Real estate agencies, this is how Bogoboom will help you grow:

  • ✅ Get matched with professionals, students and expats looking for a long-term home
  • ✅ Increase occupancy with verified leads
  • ✅ Save time with a platform built for serious tenants
  • ✅ Sign up for free and subscribe only when you're ready to scale
