# Product vision
**Positioning:** "Your smart start, anywhere." Relocation + housing in one portal; first city Bogotá.
**Segments:** BPO/tech/outsourcing/education HR teams (Teleperformance, Concentrix, Accenture, IBM, TCS, Globant, Mercado Libre…), agencies, newcomers (students, expats, remote workers).
**Pricing ideas:** Agency Free (5 credits) / Pro / Enterprise; featured listings; corporate relocation packages; affiliate deals; market data insights.
**Brand:** MIRA mascot (`site/assets/mira-avatar.png`); Colombian flag palette (yellow/blue/red) on the avatar; site uses blue `#004a8f`.
**Open questions:** pricing per market, legal entity for LATAM, verification workflow ops, agency onboarding incentives (early-bird free Pro).
