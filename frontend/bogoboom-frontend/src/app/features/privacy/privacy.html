Privacy Policy for Bogoboom

Draft for legal review. Effective date: October 27, 2026 (policy version 2026-10).

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: e-mail address and the name you choose. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider (Auth0); Bogoboom never sees or stores them.
  • Relocation profile (newcomers): city of contract, arrival date, budget, housing preferences and the contact details you or your employer's HR team provide.
  • Company and agency data: company or agency name, city, country and the work contact of the person who registers.
  • Forms: the details you send through the partnership and contact forms.
  • Technical data: IP address and request logs, kept for security and abuse prevention.

2. Who sees your data

  • Real estate agencies see a limited profile only if you opt in ("share my housing preferences with agencies"): first name, city, arrival date, budget range and preferences. They never see your e-mail or phone number unless they unlock your contact, which requires a verified agency, a profile that matches one of its listings, and a free contact credit or paid plan. Every unlock is recorded in an audit log.
  • HR teams see the profiles of the hires they register. Your employer does not decide whether agencies may contact you: that choice is yours.
  • You can withdraw agency visibility at any time from your profile; new unlocks stop immediately.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Service providers (hosting, authentication, e-mail) process it on our behalf.

3. How we use it

To provide the service (matching newcomers with housing and connecting them with HR teams and agencies), to keep it secure, to answer your requests and to meet legal obligations.

4. Your rights

You may request access, correction, export or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent, by writing to support@tecnologiasantos.com. Where Colombian data-protection law (Ley 1581 de 2012) or another law applies, you also hold the rights it grants.

5. Retention and security

We keep data while your account is active and for the period needed to meet legal duties. Data is encrypted in transit; access is limited by role and logged.

6. Cookies

Bogoboom uses only what is needed to keep you signed in. We do not run advertising trackers.

7. Changes

When this policy changes materially we publish the new version here and ask for your consent again where required.
