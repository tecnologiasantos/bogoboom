MIRA & Bogoboom

Technology from Brazil with heart, transforming lives around the world 🌍

Created by Tecnologia Santos, MIRA and Bogoboom are not just platforms. They are living dreams — born from a deep belief that technology can serve humanity in ways that touch hearts, open doors, and change destinies.

MIRA: Empowering IT Professionals Across Borders

The MIRA project is a revolutionary initiative designed to connect skilled IT professionals from every corner of the globe to international job opportunities — with dignity, fairness, and no fees attached. MIRA offers a digital home to developers, analysts, architects, and engineers. Whether you're starting out or decades into your career, this platform was made for you. For your growth. For your voice. For your future.

MIRA is more than a job portal. It is a declaration that everyone deserves opportunity, regardless of passport, location, or income. It is a movement of equality and technological justice, backed by real people and real stories.

Bogoboom: A Warm Welcome to Bogotá

Bogoboom is a dream built for newcomers — for those arriving in Bogotá full of hope, but without support. It's for the HR professional tired of dealing with endless logistics. It's for real estate agents ready to work with purpose and transparency.

Bogoboom offers more than listings. It offers peace of mind. It connects people, simplifies relocation, and empowers companies to focus on what truly matters: welcoming their teams and building culture. Through an intuitive and collaborative platform, Bogoboom bridges distance with care — ensuring that every new beginning in Bogotá starts with strength, stability, and community.

This is relocation with heart. This is the future of onboarding. This is Bogoboom.
