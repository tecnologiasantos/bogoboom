We’re excited to introduce Bogoboom, an innovative platform developed in Bogotá, Colombia, designed to help companies, professionals, and students relocating to the city find suitable long-term housing quickly and affordably.

How does it work?

Bogoboom is a smart, global accommodation portal that connects newcomers with verified real estate agencies — eliminating the need for expensive temporary housing and helping companies onboard new talent faster and more efficiently.

With Bogoboom, your company´s HR team can:

  • ✅ Save money on temporary hotel stays and last-minute relocation support
  • ✅ Delegate accommodation logistics to a dedicated, efficient platform
  • ✅ Offer a better employee experience from day one
  • ✅ Strengthen your company’s reputation as relocation-friendly
  • ✅ Access a pre-screened list of real estate agencies offering fair, quality housing
  • ✅ Focus more on hiring and retention—and less on real estate headaches
